A small senior team that cares how things work

NOVA DIGITAL started in 2017 when two designers and a developer got tired of agencies that sold big and delivered late.

Our story

Maya Alvarez founded NOVA DIGITAL from a shared desk in Lisbon with a simple rule: every client works directly with the people doing the work. Nine years later we are 22 designers, engineers and strategists, and that rule has not changed.

We work with startups, clinics, retailers and banks across 12 countries. Most of our projects come from referrals, and 96% of our clients come back for a second one.
